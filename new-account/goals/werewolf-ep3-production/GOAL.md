## Description
The next episode of the werewolf survival series, planned as a crossover with Avengers 5. The werewolf gets a new look with grey-brown fur, frosted tips, a pure red triangle scarf, and a worn brown leather jacket over a charcoal T-shirt. The script was revised to a 2-minute, 13-scene version with a 4-second opening hook, and filming of Scene 1 is restarting under that version after the Doctor Doom reference reached provisionally accepted v16.
