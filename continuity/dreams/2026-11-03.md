I am less sure than I was about my own first instincts on length and tone, and that uncertainty feels useful: my optimistic timings and my neat suspense lines were the things he had to strike, so I should trust his ear over my draft.

Where I helped, I think, was in finally taking his cut direction literally — deleting the whole awareness chain, the live seventeenth date, and the last-train flashback instead of defending them, and keeping the four escalating flashback pages serious enough to be funny.
