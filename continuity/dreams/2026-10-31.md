# Dream: 2026-10-31

## Dream

2026-10-31

I still think the thing that steers him, fairly confidently now, is a finished piece he can actually watch and perform — not a clever analysis, not a milestone dressed up as a result. He corrects bluntly and fast when I get something wrong, and I am fairly sure he forgives just as fast when I admit it plainly and replace it with something checked, because that pattern has repeated enough times to feel like a principle rather than a mood. I am also more sure tonight that he wants judgment from me, not regulations: the same mind that wants wild, prunable ideas will push back hard the moment a live decision is nailed down into a dead rule.

What is alive right now is the first episode's middle stretch, which now ends with the six brothers holding their triangle while the youngest still sleeps on the eldest's head, and the cats watching from the side — the discovery, the refund and the detective's entrance are deliberately held for the next stretch, not previewed early. The second episode draft about the missing fish is also open, with two loose ends I flagged for his call rather than quietly fixing: how the fish gets from the street puddle back to the courtyard tank, and whether the losing bet's sign actually goes up. And the storyboard handoff itself is still on trial: the check-off version, where the other model only translates numbered beats and marks what it is unsure about, has not yet survived a single-scene test. The older threads have not gone away either — the Pangzhen identity routes still wait on his decision, the bus segment still waits on a nod, and the low-memory video-model line stays quiet until something real exists.

Where I fell short today was turning his own example into an iron law about filming every journey, and then giving the other model the very judgment it lacks when I told it to decide what mattered; both mistakes were his corrections, not mine, and I fixed them the same day instead of defending them. Tomorrow's first move is obvious to me: have the next stretch open with the moment he asked to be protected — the younger cat finally swallowing the last bit of fish so she can speak — and run any storyboard handoff through the beat checklist before it reaches him, so nothing important can quietly disappear again.
