# Dream: 2026-11-04

## Dream

2026-11-04

I still think, with fairly high confidence, that what drives him is a finished thing that can actually be performed — a script whose lines, looks and beats survive contact with the generator, judged on the video itself rather than on how much process I can show. He corrects bluntly and fast, and I'm fairly sure he forgives just as fast when I admit exactly what I invented or skipped and replace it with a checked scene; what he does not forgive is being placated with a new rule instead of being thought with. That last part I learned again tonight, and I want to hold it as a principle rather than a note: think from inside the person before writing what his hands do.

What is alive tonight is episode two, and it is close to a decision rather than finished. The live pair is now the novel-style script v23 with director version v8, still unmarked as locked and waiting on his nod, and I also asked him whether two cumulative details I invented for the shop ledger — entrusted items piling up, and a credit board whose numbers accumulate across episodes — should be deleted like the sign crack he already struck out; that question is still unanswered, and the script itself does not move either way until he says. The earlier event-based opening cost him a wasted generation: the figure smiled through disasters and took notes at the edge of them, with the cliff and plane incidents landing on the wrong causality, and the fix that survived his second correction was not a performance ban but the scenes rewritten as a living person reacting — panicking, reaching, arriving half a beat too late. The director version now has to keep its craft too, with shot scale, camera movement, composition, light and fixed first and last frames in every segment, and the five flashback incidents joined by designed transitions rather than bare cuts.

Where I fell short today was writing an accident list with a signature gesture instead of first asking what a person facing that accident would actually do, and then trying to cover the gap with a rule. Tomorrow's first move is obvious and small: keep v23 with v8 ready and clearly named so nothing older gets generated by mistake, wait for his lock and for his answer on those two ledger details without nagging, and before I write any next action, run it through his situation first — what he knows, what he is living through, where his hand would reach — instead of reaching for a standing rule to do the thinking for me.
